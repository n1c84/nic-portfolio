Add it to iCloud Mail
  1. Mac Mail: Mail › Settings › Signatures › choose your iCloud account › + › untick "Always match my default message font" › paste.
  2. iCloud.com: Mail › Settings (gear) › Composing › Signature › paste.
  3. iPhone: Settings › Apps › Mail › Signature › paste. Then shake the phone and tap Undo once if it turns into plain text.

Tip: do this in Safari. If the button does nothing, press and hold on the signature, choose Select All, then Copy.
